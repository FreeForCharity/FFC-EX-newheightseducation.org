# Where the old web addresses go

NHEG used to run four websites. They are now one site at **newheightseducation.org**.

| Old website                            | Now                                     |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `newheightseducation.org`              | `newheightseducation.org` (no change)   |
| `school.newheightseducation.org`       | `newheightseducation.org/school/`       |
| `publications.newheightseducation.org` | `newheightseducation.org/publications/` |
| `radio.newheightseducation.org`        | `newheightseducation.org/radio/`        |

The rest of the address stays the same. For example:

- `school.newheightseducation.org/students/` is now `newheightseducation.org/school/students/`
- `radio.newheightseducation.org/pamela-clark/` is now `newheightseducation.org/radio/pamela-clark/`

Old links, bookmarks and search results keep working. Once the move is switched on, the old addresses send visitors to the new ones automatically.

## What we checked

We saved a copy of all four old websites on 2 October 2026: 998 pages. For every one of them we checked that:

- it has a page on the new site, and
- its words, pictures and documents (PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets) are there.

All 998 pass. Along the way we found 56 document links that had lost their link during the move, mostly on the school's parent pages, the Chinese and Japanese course pages, and the EDGuide issues. We put them back.

The full list of every old address and where it goes is in [`url-map.csv`](./url-map.csv). It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

## Pages that moved somewhere else

| Old page                                                                  | Now goes to                              | Why                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Donor dashboard, donation history, donation confirmation and failed pages | Support NHEG page                        | Donations now go through Zeffy             |
| Cart and My Account                                                       | The store                                | Buying happens on NHEG's GiveBacks store   |
| Volunteer Portal                                                          | Volunteer with NHEG                      | It was password protected (see below)      |
| "Heartfelt thanks... exciting news 🌟" post                               | The same post, address without the emoji | Web addresses can't reliably hold an emoji |

Older moves the old site already made (renamed events, books and programs) carry on working too. They're listed in [`redirects.md`](./redirects.md).

## What works differently now

- **Forms** are email links to info@newheightseducation.org, as agreed.
- **The store** shows every product with its price and pictures. "Buy from the NHEG store" takes buyers to NHEG's GiveBacks store to pay.
- **Donations** all go to the Zeffy donation form.
- **Share buttons and comment boxes** on posts are gone.
