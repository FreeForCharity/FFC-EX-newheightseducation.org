NHEG Offers Q&A Centers for Parents with School Age Children

By Michael Anderson
 Posted March 29, 2019
 In NHEG News
NHEG Offers Q&A Centers for Parents with School Age Children2019-03-292019-03-29%%BASE%%/_ffc-assets/newheightseducation.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/nheg-tree-logo-white.pngNew Heights Educational Group, Inc.%%BASE%%/_ffc-assets/newheightseducation.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/nheg-tree-logo-white.png200px200px

NHEG now offers a Q&A Center for families of school-age children.  There are three separate answer centers available 24/7. Each center costs a one-time fee of $30-$35 to access and provides answers to many education questions.

Home School Q&A Center https://School.Newheightseducation.org/parents/home-school-families/
Charter School Q&A Center https://School.NewHeightsEducation.org/parents/charter-school-families/
Public School Q&A Center https://School.NewHeightsEducation.org/parents/public-school-families/
