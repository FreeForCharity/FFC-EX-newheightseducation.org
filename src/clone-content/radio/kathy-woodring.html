History of America with Host Kathy Woodring

Kathy Woodring Bio

Kathy’s show covers the history of the United States, from the early settlements to present day, focusing on significant events. Included will be the development of the founding documents, the structure of American government on the federal and state level, and the people that make America a unique, stimulating nation. Many homeschoolers use this show towards high school credit.
