Mrs. Pamela Clark is the founder and executive director of New Heights Educational Group, a nonprofit located in northwest Ohio, and the New Heights Show on Education. With a global reach, New Heights Educational Group has earned over 90 awards and honors since its creation in June 2006. Mrs. Clark has 21 years of experience helping families with all their educational needs. She began serving families when she discovered a need for cooperation between families and the educational system, especially in educating children with learning difficulties such as ADHD, bipolar disorder, autism and neurological disorders.

With a passion for bettering education, she works tirelessly to improve the lives of families that come to her for assistance. Her team of over 20 volunteers researches the latest advancements and uses past successes to help these families. Of her work, Mrs. Clark says, “At a young age, my grandmother instilled in me a belief and a passion for bettering education and speaking up for what I believe in.” Mrs. Clark previously hosted weekly “Civil Rights” and a weekly show called “Education in the News” podcast that covers educational news stories in the United States and globally.

Pamela is currently launching another new Podcast titled “American Service Men and Women Tell Their Stories of Survival.” Hosted by Pamela Clark, this podcast will feature firsthand accounts from sailors who served aboard the USS Drexler, with plans to explore additional stories and branches of the military as well. This pre-recorded show will be available on our website starting June 25, 2025. Homeschool families are encouraged to incorporate t
his resource into their American History studies.

Her pre-recorded show airs on Wednesdays at 6 pm EST.
