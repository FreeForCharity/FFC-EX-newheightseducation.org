Depression, anxiety and other disabilities within our community with Host Erika Hanson

Erika Hanson show discusses depression, anxiety and other disabilities within our community. In Her personal struggles with Cerebral Palsy give her a unique insight into the way in which the traditional public school system handles children with disabilities, and the subject remains a life long interest for her.
